Abstract: 

> The study conducts a comprehensive evaluation of foundation models for polyp detection and segmentation in colonoscopy. The researchers compared five foundation models—DINOv2, YOLO-World, GroundingDINO, SAM, and MedSAM—against two benchmark networks (YOLOv8 and Mask R-CNN) across three different colonoscopy datasets. Results reveal that foundation models' success in polyp characterization depends heavily on domain specialization: generic models require fine-tuning to be effective, while domain-specific models achieve superior performance. Some foundation models demonstrated strong zero-shot evaluation capabilities, outperforming fine-tuned models on unseen data.
